About Us

One of Pakistan's Leading Public Universities

Established in 1951 under an act of parliament, the University of Karachi began as an examination university before starting teaching and research in 1953 with the Faculties of Arts and Science. In 1960 it moved to its present 1,279-acre campus on University Road.

Nine Faculties

Arts & Social Sciences, Science, Islamic Studies, Pharmacy, Management & Admin. Sciences, Law, Education, Medicine and Engineering.

53 Departments

The Faculty of Science alone comprises 23 departments and five research institutes.

Library & Research

The Dr. Mahmud Husain Library holds over 360,000 books, including the Quaid-e-Azam's personal collection.
