HEC / Sindh HEC Policies

The University of Karachi implements policy directives issued by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and the Sindh Higher Education Commission (Sindh HEC), covering areas from student welfare to campus conduct. Adopted policies are published here for reference by students, faculty and staff.

Policy for Students with Disabilities 2021 (Amended)

A university-wide policy supporting the access, accommodation and welfare of students with disabilities, adopted in line with HEC / Sindh HEC guidance.

Policy on Drug and Tobacco Abuse 2021

A campus-wide policy addressing the prevention of drug and tobacco abuse, adopted under HEC / Sindh HEC directives.
