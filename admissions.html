How to Apply

Applications, Entry Test & Fee Vouchers

Prospectuses and advertisements for each admission cycle are issued by the Directorate of Admissions. Most undergraduate and postgraduate programs require the University of Karachi Entry Test; processing fee vouchers can be generated and paid online via Kuick Pay or at partner bank branches.

Contact Admissions
Semester Fee

Fee Vouchers & Payment

Semester and examination fee vouchers are generated online each term and can be paid via UBL branches, Kuick Pay, or Al-Falah branches. Late fee surcharges apply after the published due date — contact the Semester Cell for queries.

FOREIGN STUDENTS

International Applicants Welcome

An affordable fee structure and a wide diversity of degree programs attract foreign students from developing and developed countries every year. The Foreign Students' Advisor Office guides applicants through the admission process and supports them once enrolled.
