UNIVERSITY OF KARACHI ALUMNI

Stay Connected With Your Alma Mater

The first official Alumni body of the University of Karachi

The University of Karachi Alumni (UKA) brings together graduates from all academic programmes at UoK and its affiliated colleges — Bachelors, Masters, MS/MPhil and PhD — across Social and Biological Sciences, Management and Administrative Sciences, Pharmacy, Islamic Studies, Law, Medicine, Engineering and Education. It is an ever-growing body representing a huge reservoir of diverse talent and expertise, and the bond students form with their alma mater strengthens further once they become alumni.

A Global Network

UKA connects alumni around the world, no matter where their careers have taken them.

Office in the Public Relations Office

Administrative facilities are provided for alumni membership completion and coordination.

Registration Open

The University has launched alumni registration; forms are available through the alumni office.

GIVE BACK

Support Your Alma Mater

The University of Karachi Endowment Fund channels alumni generosity into scholarships for deserving students, faculty development and campus improvement. Alumni can support the University in several ways:

Endowment Fund

Contribute directly to the UoK Endowment Fund, which finances scholarships for needy and deserving students and university development projects.

Mentorship & the Brain Bank

Join UKA's "Brain Bank" of alumni professionals — mentor current students, host internships, or speak at departmental events.

Scholarship Sponsorship

Sponsor a scholarship in your name or your batch's name to help a deserving student complete their degree.

AIMS & OBJECTIVES

Building a Cohesive Alumni Network

Through this alumni body, the University ensures good communication between alumni and the University itself — timely announcement of University events, and different alumni-based activities that help connect graduates around the globe. These measures keep alumni informed about fellow graduates' achievements and important developments at the University.

  • Promote communication between old students and the University
  • Support the University's mission of quality education through its alumni
  • Build a "Brain Bank" of professionals drawn from the alumni body
  • Organize scholarship funds for needy and deserving students

Get in Touch With UKA

For membership queries, registration forms and updates on alumni activities, reach out to the alumni office.
