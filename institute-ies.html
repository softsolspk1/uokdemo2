Institute of Environmental Studies

Established in 1982, the Institute of Environmental Studies (IES) is the oldest and pioneering institute in the field of environment in Pakistan, offering M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees. The Institute began offering M.Sc., Postgraduate Diploma and Certificate courses in 1996, and a B.S. program in 2009. It is recognized as a technical Focal Point on Environmental Health Activities of WHO/EMRO.

  • B.S. / M.Sc. / M.Phil. / Ph.D. Programs
  • Environmental Laboratories
  • WHO/EMRO Technical Focal Point
  • Postgraduate Diploma & Certificate Courses
