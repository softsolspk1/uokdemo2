Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Chair

The Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Chair was set up at the University of Karachi on 12 November 2008. Records available from the University indicate the Chair's early leadership history but do not include a detailed public description of its current programs — for the latest information, please contact the University directly.

  • Established: 12 November 2008
  • Research & Advisory Chair
  • University of Karachi

Leadership History

  • Director: Ms. Sahar Gul Bhatti (appointed 01/06/2015)
  • Mr. Aijaz Qureshi appointed Director — 24/06/2011
  • Prof. M. Saleem Memon appointed Professor/Advisor — 29/07/2011
  • Mr. Mazhar-ul-Siddique (former VC, University of Sindh Jamshoro) selected as an expert member of the Advisory Committee — 29/07/2011
