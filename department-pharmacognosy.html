Department of Pharmacognosy

Faculty of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences

Pharmacognosy — from the Greek "pharmakon" (drug) and "gnosis" (knowledge) — is the study of natural bioactive substances from plants, animals, microbes and minerals, an integral part of the Faculty of Pharmacy since its founding in 1973.

* FACULTY OF PHARMACY * Natural Product Research

Our Focus

Natural Products & Phytochemistry

Phytochemistry

Extraction, separation, purification and structure elucidation of natural products.

Ethno-pharmacology

Medicinal value, distribution and biosynthetic pathways of plant drugs.

Nutraceuticals

Standardization of nutraceuticals and cosmeceuticals from natural sources.

Today it has grown into a well-established department that forms the backbone of pharmacy education in the Faculty of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences — a highly interdisciplinary field drawing on botany, organic chemistry, biochemistry, pharmacology, zoology and ethno-medicine. The Department assists undergraduates toward a Pharm.D. degree and guides young investigators toward M.Phil./PhD research in Pharmacognosy.

Careers

Career Opportunities

Graduates work across the pharmaceutical industry (manufacturing, quality control, drug development), hospital and clinical pharmacy, regulatory bodies, academia, and research and development — with a particular focus on modernizing Pakistan's herbal drug industry on a scientific basis.

  • An integral part of the Faculty of Pharmacy since 1973.
  • Strong national interest in medicinal plants and traditional (Unani/Homeopathic) medicine informs research.
  • Prepares graduates for Pharm.D., M.Phil. and PhD pathways.
